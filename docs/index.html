The supervisor chooses freely

The supervisor sees a vague brief, never the hidden answer. It may choose any OpenRouter-supported effort, including zero reasoning, then the worker makes three attempts at that choice. Three independent supervisor choices create the other matrix axis.

All seven levels are selectable. The benchmark rewards choosing the lowest safe one, not choosing a fashionable level.
